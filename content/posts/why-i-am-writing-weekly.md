For eight years I've shipped software in payments, education and AI, and most of what I learnt along the way stayed in my head, in pull request descriptions, or in decision records nobody outside the team will read.

So starting this week, I'm writing one post a week about something I learnt or studied in software engineering. Some weeks it will be a deep dive. Some weeks it will be a short note. The point is to keep a record and to think more clearly by writing things down.

## What to expect

- **Payments and money systems**: ledgers, idempotency, reconciliation, and what breaks when real money moves.
- **Using AI safely in production**: where a model should read facts, and where plain code should make the decision.
- **Engineering practice**: code review, incident response, and writing down decisions and their trade-offs.
